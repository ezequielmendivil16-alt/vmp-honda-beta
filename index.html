VMP-001 METHOD

What this beta is — and is not

Purpose

Put a real, shareable product in front of Honda owners now, gather feedback, and improve VMP while the broader vehicle database continues to grow.

Publication rule

Only annual rows marked PUBLISHED are selectable. PARTIAL, VERIFIED_PROFILE_PENDING_ANNUAL and BLOCKED_SOURCE entries remain hidden from testers.

Maintenance timing

Honda uses Maintenance Minder for most normal service timing. This beta does not convert those codes into made-up universal mileage intervals.

VIN limitation

VIN decoding is not implemented yet. The VIN field is optional and local-only; the tester confirms the year, family and mechanical profile manually.

Privacy

No personal vehicle is hard-coded. Browser state stays local unless the tester chooses to copy or download a feedback report.

Safety

This is a beta maintenance organizer, not a substitute for the owner's manual, Maintenance Minder display, service information, recalls or professional diagnosis.
